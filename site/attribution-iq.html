02 The answer

The article that started the sale still gets 40% of the credit.

Last click gives the checkout page 100. Attribution IQ splits a six-step path 40, 20, 40: the first touch, the middle, the last touch. Multi-touch coefficients rank every asset by how much it moved the people who saw it.

What this means. A publisher walks into the renewal with the path and the credit, not a last-click report.

03 What you get

Three things on every Attribution IQ file.

01

Asset and phase.

Which units moved people, in which week. Trailer, article, creator, display, owned. The campaign as it actually ran.

02

Paid and organic.

Organic often forms the demand and paid often closes it. Both are on the same person, so the relay is visible.

03

Multi-touch credit.

One coefficient per touchpoint, ranked. First, middle, and last credit on every conversion, updated every day the campaign is live.
