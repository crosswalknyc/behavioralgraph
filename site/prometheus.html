02 What it is built on

Behavioral data on 30 million digital lives.

Not a survey and not a model. Crosswalk holds the record of what real people do online: 30 million opted-in people around the world, 10 million of them in a US panel that is census matched to the country. For each person, their entire digital life across every screen, under one ID, kept day by day since January 2021. That record is what Prometheus answers from.

Behavioral
What people search, watch, stream, buy, subscribe to and cancel. Observed, not asked.
Longitudinal
The same people in the panel day to day and year after year. Their behavioral data evolves daily and the record of their history remains intact.
Every screen
Phone, laptop, connected TV, tablet, game console. One person, one ID, one ordinary day in order with the time of day.
Projected
Every answer scales from the panel to the US population the way a census sample does, in percentages and in people.

09 Questions

Prometheus, in plain words.

What is Prometheus?

Crosswalk's research assistant. You ask a question about an audience in plain English, and it answers from the behavior of 10 million US people in the Crosswalk panel: what they search, watch, stream, buy, subscribe to and cancel, recorded day by day since 2021.

What can I ask it?

Anything about an audience. Who watches a title and what they buy. Which titles bring a service new subscribers. How people got from a clip to a purchase. Which touchpoint on a campaign moved them. What a sponsorship is worth. The nine syndicated reports are the menu, not the limit.

How fast is it?

A quick count comes back in seconds. A full read, such as a complete audience profile, takes a few minutes and lands in your dashboard as a file you can download or email.

How is this different from a survey or a ratings service?

Surveys tell you what people say. Ratings see one screen, web analytics one site, card data one checkout. Prometheus reads one person across all of it, ten million times over, census matched and refreshed every morning, and its answers are checked against real outcomes rather than more surveys.

What does it cost?

You open a dashboard with a $500 opening balance. A quick lookup is about 15 cents, a detailed analysis about 84 cents, and a full Profile IQ report is $300. You pay only for the reports you pull.

Is any personal data used or sold?

No. The panel is opted in, identity is kept apart from behavior, and Prometheus never answers about one person. Clients receive group statistics projected to the US population.
