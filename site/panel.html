02 Two layers

Your record stays yours. Clients get the group.

The panel record and the client file are two different things, kept apart on purpose. Identity keys sit away from the behavioral log.

Layer A The panel record

What we hold to run the panel.

Identity, the device data needed to operate the software, and the clickstream. Used to run the panel and to honor your rights. Sensitive query parameters are redacted before anything is stored.

Layer B What clients receive

Group statistics, in the dashboard.

Penetration, index against the US population, projected US counts, ranked brands. Not a list of people. We do not sell raw clickstream and we will not re-identify a file.
