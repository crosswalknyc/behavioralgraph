02 The answer

The Pitt Season 2 brought HBO Max 217,278 signups, and most were returning accounts.

82,567 new accounts and 134,711 returning ones opened or came back within 30 days of a Season 2 play, then watched. Viewers spend 26 extra minutes a week on HBO Max.
