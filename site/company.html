03 How we write the numbers

Three words appear on every page we ship.

213

Index.

100 is the US general population. 213 means this audience is about twice as likely to do the thing. Never written with a percent sign.

21.8%

Penetration.

The share of the audience that did it, one decimal, always with the base stated. About 1 in 5 in this case.

3.6M

Projected.

Weighted to the US population. 10 million US panelists stand in for the country, and every projected figure carries its sample.

What this means. We do not sell raw clickstream. A shipped file is group statistics that cannot reasonably be linked back to a person. That commitment is public.
