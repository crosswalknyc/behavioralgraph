03 How we write the numbers

Data you can trust.

Zero Party.

Our data is ethically sourced in partnership with blue chip brand clients to ensure a provenance chain that passes compliance with even the toughest hedge funds and banks.

Compliant.

PII is all redacted and removed before it ever even hits our server. Data is all non-identifiable and the privacy and protection of our panel is of the utmost importance.

Gen Pop.

Weighted to the US population. 10 million US panelists stand in for the country, and every projected figure carries its sample.

What this means. We do not sell customer files. Our aggregate data is group statistics that cannot reasonably be linked back to a person.
