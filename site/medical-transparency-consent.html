Medical information transparency consent

Last updated: 28 August 2026

This page states what Crosswalk Technologies, Inc. may observe about health-related digital behavior on the panel, and what clients receive. It is part of panel consent, with the Privacy Policy, Tracking consent transparency, and Terms of Use.

If you do not agree, do not install the software, or uninstall it and send a deletion request.

What this consent covers

When you join the panel, clickstream may include visits to pharmacies, insurers, health systems, wellness brands, and health-information sites. That is digital behavior on sites you chose to open. It is Layer A.

We use it to operate the panel and to build aggregated audience products (Layer B), for example how an audience indexes on a pharmacy, an insurer, or a wellness brand.

Crosswalk is not a HIPAA covered entity or a business associate. We do not collect protected health information from your providers. This page is not a HIPAA authorization and is not a waiver of HIPAA rights.

What we do not collect

We do not request or receive your medical history from healthcare providers, facilities, plans, clearinghouses, or business associates. We do not collect diagnoses, lab results, treatment notes, or billing records from those parties. We do not collect genetic data as a product. We do not collect Social Security numbers.

A shipped client file never contains consumer health data as a product, a biometric identifier, or you as a row.

What clients receive

Clients receive de-identified, aggregated group statistics. They may see that an audience indexes high on a pharmacy, a health brand, or an insurer. They do not receive your health record. They do not receive you as a row.

We do not sell your personal information as an individual record. We do not sell your data as raw clickstream. We do not use Layer A or Layer B to determine eligibility for credit, insurance, employment, or housing. Clients receive it only in our aggregated dashboard products. We will not re-identify it. De-identified data.

Where a state requires opt-in to process sensitive personal information on Layer A, we obtain it before that processing. We do not sell sensitive personal information.

Age

The panel is for adults by default. Under-18 collection requires parental or guardian consent, and only where legal. Under 13 requires verifiable parental consent. See COPPA and Parental consent.
