Download the panel app for Mac or Windows.

The app is free. It explains what it collects before you install it. Pick the computer you use.

Download for Mac Download for Windows
Mac
Double-click the installer. It works on Intel and Apple Silicon. No extra warning.
Windows
On the first open, Windows may say it protected your PC. Click More info, then Run anyway.
After install
A registration window opens once. Then the app sits in the menu bar on Mac, or the system tray on Windows, and starts when you log in.
