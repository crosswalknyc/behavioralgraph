02 The answer

Nine in ten regular viewers who watched Gilmore Girls went on to another Amazon surface.

5.19 million regular Prime Video accounts watched the title in the window. 4.72 million touched a second Amazon-owned surface afterward, and 2.84 million completed a checkout. Another 870,386 lapsed accounts came back for it, and 548,261 were new to Prime Video.

What this means. A title's value to the company is the whole wheel, not the play.
