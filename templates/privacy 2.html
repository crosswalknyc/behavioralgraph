Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 14, 2025

Your Privacy Matters: Crosswalk Technologies, Inc. is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our Behavioral Graph platform.

1. Introduction

Crosswalk Technologies, Inc. ("Crosswalk," "we," "us," or "our") operates the Crosswalk Behavioral Graph platform (the "Service"). This Privacy Policy applies to information we collect through our Service, including our website, applications, and related services.

By using our Service, you consent to the data practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with these practices, please do not use our Service.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

We collect information you voluntarily provide when you:

  • Create an Account: Username, email address, password, and professional information
  • Use Our Service: Search queries, analysis parameters, and preferences
  • Contact Us: Name, email, and message content when you reach out for support
  • Subscribe to Communications: Email address and communication preferences

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

When you access our Service, we automatically collect:

  • Device Information: IP address, browser type, operating system, and device identifiers
  • Usage Data: Pages visited, features used, time spent on Service, and interaction patterns
  • Log Data: Server logs recording requests, errors, and system events
  • Cookies and Tracking: Session cookies and analytics identifiers

2.3 Analytics Data We Process

Our Service processes aggregated and anonymized consumer insights data. This data:

  • Does not contain personally identifiable information
  • Is sourced from licensed third-party data providers
  • Is aggregated at population level for statistical analysis
  • Cannot be used to identify specific individuals

To exercise these rights, contact us at privacy@crosswalknyc.com or call 1-800-CROSSWALK.
