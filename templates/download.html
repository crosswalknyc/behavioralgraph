Trouble opening on macOS?

If macOS shows "Apple could not verify CrosswalkIQ-Mac.pkg is free of malware", that's Gatekeeper's default warning for apps not yet notarized by Apple. The installer is safe; here's how to open it:

  1. Click Done on the warning (not "Move to Trash").
  2. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security.
  3. Scroll to the bottom. You'll see a line about the blocked file with an Open Anyway button. Click it and authenticate with Touch ID or your password.
  4. Double-click CrosswalkIQ-Mac.pkg again. Click Open on the confirmation dialog and the installer will run normally.
Trouble opening on Windows?

If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC — Microsoft Defender SmartScreen prevented an unrecognized app from starting", that's the default warning for apps not yet signed with a Microsoft-recognized certificate. The installer is safe; here's how to open it:

  1. Click More info on the SmartScreen dialog.
  2. Click Run anyway at the bottom.
  3. The installer will proceed normally.
