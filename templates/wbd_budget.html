How to budget Prometheus.

Two lines in the plan: the $120,000 dashboard license and a metered report line sized from your planned slate.

Size the report line from your planned slate.

Start with the titles, audiences, and talent you expect to study. One Profile IQ at $300 is the base: 100 profiles is $30,000 over the term.

Pull campaign reports only when a campaign is live.

Brand Partnership, Digital Journey IQ, and Subscriber Acquisition are $500 each and belong to a specific title or campaign. No title needs every report type.

Treat the balance as cash flow, not a fee.

The $5,000 opening balance, the $5,000 top-up at $500 remaining, and the usage statement are one mechanism. Every dollar charged is a dollar of reports your team pulled or will pull.

Set the in-flight window before an Attribution IQ campaign starts.

Days of in-flight updates are the lever: $500 to set up plus $100 per day. A 14-day window is $1,900, a 30-day window is $3,500. Decide the window with the team running the campaign.

Name one owner for the usage statement.

Charges are downloadable from the dashboard at any time. One person reviewing it keeps spend matched to the plan and catches duplicate pulls early.

Budget chat by the question, not the token.

Chat is billed on the text Prometheus reads and writes: $10.50 per million tokens read, $52.50 per million written, plus $0.021 per search. A million tokens is about 750,000 words. In practice a quick lookup runs about $0.15 and a detailed analysis about $0.84.

Start a fresh chat for a new topic.

Prometheus rereads the conversation on every turn, so long threads cost a little more per question. Chat is a small line next to report pulls, and a new thread for each title or campaign keeps it that way.

Review the run rate daily, weekly, and quarterly.

Check daily charges in the usage statement, compare weekly totals with the plan in this model, and each quarter resize the report line for the rest of the term as titles and campaigns come in.
