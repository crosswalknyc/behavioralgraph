Crosswalk Profile IQ

How we define the sample & behavior windows

How Profile IQ usually builds audiences in 2025, and what changes when we need more panelists to reach our minimum sample size.

Internal reference Typical study year: 2025 Minimum Crosswalk respondents: 100,000

Two paths, one rule for behavior

Who we include in the profile (the sample) can widen backward in time if needed. What we count as engagement (behavior) stays inside the study year unless your run specifies otherwise. When we extend the panel to reach 100,000 for behavior, the US projection on the dashboard still reflects the original study year—not that larger behavioral sample.

Most profiles

Standard window

Enough people were active in 2025 on their own.

  • Sample: panelists active in 2025
  • Behavior: only their digital activity in 2025
  • Sample and behavior use the same calendar window
When 2025 alone is too small

Extended recruitment

2025 might only yield e.g. 40k people—but we still need 100k for stable behavior modeling.

  • Behavior sample: keep adding earlier cohorts (2024, 2023, …) until we reach ≥ 100,000 panelists (2025 behaviors only)
  • US projection: uses the 2025-only audience size—the real count from the original year, not the 100k used for behavior
  • Older panelists count for membership, not for pre-2025 behavior

🔁 Decision flow

What the pipeline is doing conceptually—not every profile uses extended lookback.

1

Start with 2025

Look for panelists who meet the profile definition with sample and behavior both anchored to the study year (typically full-year 2025).

2

Count 2025 Crosswalk respondents

How many matched panelists qualified in the study year alone? If already ≥ 100,000 → standard path. If under → extended path (but note the original 2025 count for US projection).

3

If not, extend recruitment backward

Include panelists from earlier years who still qualify for the profile, until cumulative respondents ≥ 100,000.

4

Behavior filter unchanged

For every person in the behavioral sample—whether they joined in 2025 or were recruited from an earlier year—only 2025 behaviors are used for engagement and insights.

5

US projection = original year only

If 2025 started below 100,000, the dashboard US figure is projected from that original 2025 cohort—not from the 100k behavioral sample. Crosswalk n may show ≥ 100k; US reflects real-world scale for the study year.

Summary

Profile IQ profiles are usually built from panelists active in 2025, using only their 2025 digital behavior. When a niche audience cannot reach 100,000 people in 2025 alone, we widen who we recruit by pulling in qualified panelists from earlier years until the behavioral sample hits 100k—but we still only score their 2025 activity. The US projection on the profile stays anchored to the original 2025 cohort (however many that was), not to the 100k we use for behavior.

  • Date range on the dashboard reflects the behavior / study window (e.g. 2025-01-01 – 2025-12-31).
  • Crosswalk respondents can show ≥ 100k after extended recruitment (for modeling stability).
  • US projection reflects the study-year audience when the profile initially had < 100k—not the enlarged behavioral sample.
  • Extended lookback is a sample-size fix for behavior, not a change to study year or US scale.

“We go back in time to get 100k for behavior, but US scale is always what 2025 alone actually represents—not the 100k headcount.”
