Viewers added creator content without giving up movies and series.
Creatorverse viewers make up 23% of Tubi’s audience. They account for 41% of everything watched on Tubi, so the hub reached the platform’s heaviest viewers first.
Creator content is only 14.2% of what those viewers watch on Tubi. Movies, series and live still account for 85.8%.
Kinigra Deon reaches more Creatorverse viewers than MrBeast.
She launched with a deep episodic library. The MrBeast seasons arrived in August 2025, a month into the year we measured, and came over as back catalog. Mythical sits third at 19.8%.
The YouTube question
Tubi is not draining YouTube.
78.9% of Creatorverse viewers watch YouTube weekly or more, and 61.2% watched the same creator on both platforms. Their YouTube time held roughly flat all year.
Rather than pulling people away from YouTube, Tubi gives them creator content on the television, and earns the ad time that comes with it.
Small share of TV time, outsized engagement
YouTube holds 14.3% of all US TV time. Tubi holds 2.3%. Creatorverse is about 0.2%. This is not a share fight with YouTube and it does not need to be.
Creatorverse viewers open Tubi 9.8 days a month, against 6.4 for other Tubi viewers. Some of that gap was there already, because the heaviest viewers are the first to try anything new. But compare the same people before and after their first Creatorverse view and their viewing still rose.
