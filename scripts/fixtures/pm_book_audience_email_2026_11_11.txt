THE SPLIT BY SOURCE

Each source counts a person once inside that source, so the sources add to more than the year's total, which counts each person once. Every source sits at or below its year's total. The full split for every year is in the file.

A search or a video about a title after its film was announced is on the same title and is counted; it cannot be split between the book and the film.

YOUR TWO QUESTIONS

What we do and do not capture. The data is the digital activity of 10 million US consumers who opted in, measured across the devices they use, phones, computers and connected TVs included, on the same ID. TikTok and YouTube viewing on a phone is counted like everything else, once per person. What sits outside is anything with no digital trace: a copy bought in a store, borrowed or passed along is not in the books row.

Whether any figure is filled in rather than counted. No. Each figure is a count of people observed doing exactly what its line says inside the window, projected to the US from the same base; nothing is filled in from another audience or carried over from a different window.
